FAQ

Got
Questions?

Is there a contract?
No contracts — ever. Your membership is month-to-month. You can cancel at any time without penalties or fees.
What is the enrollment fee for?
The $100 enrollment fee is a one-time charge that covers your key FOB, equipment orientation, and onboarding. There are no annual maintenance fees or hidden charges.
Can I visit before joining?
Absolutely. We encourage it. Come by during staffed hours (Monday–Friday, 10am–8pm) for a personal tour of the facility. Call us at 586.354.9966 to arrange your visit.
Is the club really accessible 24/7?
Yes — all members receive a secure key FOB that grants access 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, including holidays. The entire facility is monitored with video surveillance.
Do I need to be an experienced lifter to join?
Not at all. Every new member receives a complimentary equipment orientation, and our knowledgeable staff is available during staffed hours for ongoing support. We welcome all fitness levels.
Is parking available?
Yes, free parking is available at our Sterling Heights location at 5742 15 Mile Rd.
